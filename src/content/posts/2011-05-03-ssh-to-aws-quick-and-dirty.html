Got your EC2 instance running? Here’s how I connect from OS X. Copy the private key you downloaded when you created the instance into your .ssh directory and restrict its file permissions. When you connect, give SSH the path to that key with -i. The final line shows a sample Ubuntu login. Use the username for your instance’s image and the hostname for your own instance.

  1. open terminal
  2. cp Downloads/somthing.pem .ssh/
  3. chmod 0600 .ssh/somthing.pem

Now, if you go ahead and try and ssh into your EC2 you will get an error. Why? Becouse you're not using the file you just copied over. Add -i somthing.pem. For example, replace the placeholders with your key file and your instance’s hostname:

ssh -i App1.pem ubuntu@ec2-40-17-31-130.compute-2.amazonaws.com
